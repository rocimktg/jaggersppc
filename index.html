Selected Case Studies

A National Sign Company’s First Paid Search Program

Google Ads, B2B lead generation · Federal Heath · May 2024 to May 2025

$647 CPLto land the first enterprise lead

QTS Data Centers, a multibillion-dollar data center operator sourcing signage across its properties.

The problem

Federal Heath had never run paid search. The first months looked like a win — cost per click fell from $6.85 to $1.68 by September 2024, the cheapest the account would ever be, and the least useful, because broad match was buying volume from searches that were never going to end in a national signage contract.

What I did

  • Moved broad match to phrase match, then sustained negative-keyword management off the search-terms report
  • Removed Search Partners and filtered invalid traffic, July 2024
  • Dayparted to 7am–7pm CST on weekdays, plus a 20% bid increase Tuesday through Thursday
  • Pruned four underperforming keywords, including a brand collision spending budget against a same-named competitor in Seattle
  • Found the measurement gap — 9 HubSpot sessions against 114 Google Ads clicks in August 2024 — and rebuilt the chain: Ads to GTM to GA4 to HubSpot form submissions

Result

CPC deliberately climbed back to $7.19 by April 2025 as the account narrowed to enterprise terms that could actually convert. Budget averaged under $800 a month. Campaign CTR held at 6–7% across the year, three keywords above 13.5%. In April 2025 the program landed QTS Data Centers at roughly $647 per qualified lead.

Comet Cleaners

Paid search, national franchise account · sourced by the Federal Heath program · 2025

160+location franchise, sourced inbound

The problem

Comet Cleaners runs 160-plus locations nationally — a category that had always bought through relationships and trade shows.

What happened

  • No prior relationship, no referral, no outbound touch — no salesperson in the loop until the form was already filled
  • Match-type and negative-keyword work on the Federal Heath account meant the only clicks it bought came from companies operating at national-vendor scale

Result

A national franchise account, inbound, off a budget under $800 a month. The account team estimated it as a six-figure recurring annual revenue stream across signage and maintenance.

Landing Page & Conversion Rebuild

Independent practice, 2025 to present · Lone Star Institute of Taekwondo, Arlington TX

2.5xform-fill leads, year over year

The problem

A local service business needed paid search, local search, and lead capture — landing pages and ad creative built in-house, on a budget that couldn’t afford waste.

What I did

  • Stripped the conversion path down to a single step
  • Cut the intake form to the fields the school actually needed
  • Moved the offer above the fold on mobile, where the traffic was

Result

Form-fill leads rose 2.5x against the same period a year earlier. The on-page work took the school to the top organic ranking for “Taekwondo Arlington TX.”

Kubota Tractor Corporation

Organic search · national equipment manufacturer · Federal Heath

3xdealer-show revenue, 2022 to 2024

The problem

Kubota needed a national account and had no organic presence built around it.

What I did

  • Built vertical-intent landing pages and long-tail national-signage content, so the query and the conversion were the same sentence
  • Optimized the pages to rank for “national signage company” in content, structure, and metadata — off the same SEMrush and competitor research as the paid account, aimed at organic
  • Ran campaign strategy and sales enablement for the annual dealer conference

Result

Marketing attributed the lead, sales closed a multi-year national account. Like Comet, it arrived inbound through search — no prior relationship, no salesperson in the loop until the deal was already moving. Connect 2024 produced 65 qualified leads and show revenue rose from $500K in 2022 to $1.5M in 2024.
