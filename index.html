Selected Case Studies

A National Sign Company’s First Paid Search Program

Google Ads, B2B lead generation · Federal Heath · May 2024 to May 2025

$647to source a qualified enterprise lead · QTS Data Centers

Federal Heath had never run paid search. The first months looked like a win: cost per click fell from $6.85 to $1.68 by September 2024, the cheapest the account would ever be, and the least useful, because broad match was buying volume from searches that were never going to end in a national signage contract. Over the rest of the year I pushed CPC deliberately back up to $7.19 by April 2025, as the account narrowed to the enterprise terms it could actually convert. The budget averaged under $800 a month, which forced the discipline; the levers, across the year:

  • Broad to phrase match, then sustained negative-keyword management off the search-terms report
  • Search Partners removed and invalid traffic filtered out, July 2024
  • Dayparting to 7am–7pm CST on weekdays, plus a 20% bid increase Tuesday through Thursday
  • Four underperforming keywords pruned, including a brand collision spending budget against a same-named competitor in Seattle

The measurement had a hole in it: in August 2024, HubSpot recorded 9 paid sessions against 114 Google Ads clicks. I flagged the gap and rebuilt the chain by October, Ads to GTM to GA4 to HubSpot form submissions, so leads could finally be traced to the campaigns that produced them.

Campaign CTR held at 6–7% across the year, with three keywords above 13.5%. In April 2025 the program produced a qualified inbound lead from QTS Data Centers, a multibillion-dollar data center operator sourcing signage across its properties, at roughly $647.

Comet Cleaners

Paid search, national franchise account · sourced by the Federal Heath program · 2025

160+location national franchise, sourced inbound

Comet Cleaners runs 160-plus locations nationally. They reached Federal Heath through the search campaign: no prior relationship, no referral, no outbound touch, no salesperson in the loop until the form was already filled. A category that had always bought through relationships and trade shows produced a national franchise account inbound, off a budget under $800 a month, because the match-type and negative-keyword work meant the only clicks the account bought came from companies operating at national-vendor scale. The account team estimated it as a six-figure recurring annual revenue stream across signage and maintenance.

Landing Page & Conversion Rebuild

Independent practice, 2025 to present · Lone Star Institute of Taekwondo, Arlington TX

2.5×form-fill leads, year over year

Since 2025 I’ve run an independent practice for local service businesses: paid search, local search, and lead capture, with the landing pages and ad creative built in-house. For Lone Star, that meant one stripped-down conversion path, an intake form cut to the fields the school actually needed, and the offer moved above the fold on mobile where the traffic was. Form-fill leads rose 2.5× against the same period a year earlier, and the on-page work took the school to the top organic ranking for “Taekwondo Arlington TX.”

Kubota Tractor Corporation

Organic search · national equipment manufacturer · Federal Heath

3×dealer-show revenue, 2022 to 2024

Vertical-intent landing pages and long-tail national-signage content brought Kubota in through organic search; the query and the conversion were the same sentence. The pages were built to rank for “national signage company” in content, structure, and metadata, worked off the same SEMrush and competitor research the paid account used, only aimed at organic. Marketing attributed the lead; sales closed a multi-year national account. Like Comet, it arrived inbound through search, with no prior relationship and no salesperson in the loop until the deal was already moving. I then ran campaign strategy and sales enablement for the annual dealer conference, where Connect 2024 produced 65 qualified leads and show revenue rose from $500K in 2022 to $1.5M in 2024.
